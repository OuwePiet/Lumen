# VIA Home Signature

Status: approved visual direction, implementation pending final asset.

## Homepage signature

The VIA homepage may use one oversized architectural background signature. It is not a conventional watermark and must never compete with content.

- The signature must contain a recognisable **V, I and A**. The I may not disappear between the outer forms.
- Scale: intentionally page-large on desktop, with controlled cropping at viewport edges.
- Mobile: use a responsive crop of the same signature rather than a second unrelated mark.
- Appearance: very thin muted VIA-green/graphite lines over the near-black background.
- Contrast must remain extremely low: discoverable on closer inspection, not a foreground logo.
- No neon, glow, animation, flashing, heavy shadow or bright fill.
- Content, NFT media, navigation and accessibility contrast always take priority.

## Brand mark

The large homepage signature is separate from the definitive VIA logo.

- The definitive custom VIA logo belongs at the upper left with `viadeso.online` nearby.
- Do not treat the current typographic VIA wordmark or NFT watermark as the final logo.
- Do not enlarge the existing NFT watermark to create the homepage signature.
- Final logo artwork remains a separate visual approval track.

## Homepage composition direction

The approved direction keeps the dark global VIA atmosphere, restrained green accents, clear primary actions, NFT discovery and a compact lower information area. A small cumulative **Total visitors** meter belongs beside the world-clock area once a reliable aggregate counting source exists. Never display invented/demo visitor totals in production.

## Implementation boundary

This document records visual intent only. Do not replace the current homepage with generated mockup imagery and do not imply mockup-only functionality is live. Implement the signature as a lightweight responsive UI asset after the final V/I/A geometry is approved.
