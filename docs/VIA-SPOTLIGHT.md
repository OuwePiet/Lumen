# VIA Spotlight

VIA Spotlight is VIA's quiet, clearly labelled self-service promotion area. It must support VIA financially without taking over the visitor experience.

## Presentation

- No pop-ups, autoplay audio/video or intrusive banner behaviour.
- Paid placements are always labelled `Sponsored`.
- Organic VIA recommendations may use `Discover`; paid placement never buys organic ranking.
- Spotlight may feature a creator, NFT, project, event or commercial sponsor.

## Self-service by default

The normal flow must not require routine manual administration by the VIA owner:

1. Advertiser composes a campaign.
2. VIA classifies the campaign automatically.
3. Automated safety and link checks run.
4. Exact price is shown before payment.
5. Advertiser sees a preview.
6. Payment is completed.
7. Eligible campaign is scheduled and published automatically.
8. VIA monitors it and expires it automatically.

Suspicious, unsafe or unclear campaigns should be paused/refused rather than silently published. Exceptional security/technical cases may be logged for investigation without creating routine daily moderation work.

## Classification

Categories:

- Community / Event
- DeSo Creator / NFT
- Business / Commercial

A DeSo account does not by itself qualify for creator/community pricing. Likewise, a project-style account name does not make an account a business. Classification should use the promoted content, destination and appropriate public account/activity signals. Genuine small or new creators must not be excluded simply because they have few followers.

Commercial shops, services, brands and business campaigns use the commercial category even when operated through a DeSo account. Repeated attempts to obtain creator pricing for the same commercial destination can be treated as circumvention.

## Draft price table

These amounts remain product pricing and can be revised transparently as VIA's reach and operating costs develop.

| Placement | 7 days | 30 days |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Community / Event | EUR 1 | EUR 3 |
| DeSo Creator / NFT | EUR 2 | EUR 6 |
| Business / Commercial | EUR 10 | EUR 30 |
| Prominent placement surcharge | +EUR 10 | +EUR 25 |

There is no `from` price: the total must be calculable and visible before payment.

Customer-facing payment options are EUR, USD and Bitcoin. DESO is not an advertising payment currency. EUR is the base tariff; USD and Bitcoin amounts are calculated at checkout using a current rate and the exact payable amount is shown before confirmation.

## Renewal

- Automatic renewal is optional and OFF by default.
- When enabled, show the next period, current renewal price and payment timing before renewal.
- A changed price must be disclosed in advance.
- Failed payment pauses/ends the placement; do not create debt or collection claims.
- The advertiser can disable renewal from My Spotlights.

## Separation from VIA Repeat

VIA Repeat is a separate low-cost creator/community visibility feature for existing creator posts and own NFT content. Business advertising is never eligible for Repeat and must use Spotlight.

## Production boundary

This document defines product policy. Payment collection, refunds, tax/legal handling, fraud controls, exchange-rate handling, automated moderation and publication must not be presented as live until their implementations are reviewed and tested. VIA never silently signs blockchain transactions.
