# VIA Daily News

VIA Daily News is a separate full-page environment at `/news`.

## Homepage rule

The VIA homepage stays clean. It may contain only a quiet navigation link/button to Daily News. Headlines, news cards, tickers and story feeds do not belong on the homepage.

## News page

The dedicated page may contain:

- World
- Technology
- DeSo & Crypto
- Art & Culture
- Music
- Science
- later: language/country selection, search and visitor preferences

## Editorial/source boundary

VIA should not republish complete third-party news articles. Use source attribution, concise original summaries and links to the original publisher. News, community content and Sponsored content must remain clearly distinguishable.

Live source integrations should be added only after source quality, attribution, update frequency, copyright/licensing and failure behaviour have been reviewed.
