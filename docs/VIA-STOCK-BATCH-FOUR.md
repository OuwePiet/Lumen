# VIA stock review — batch four

Status: community and social-personalisation ideas from historical Lumen/VIA stock, checked against viadeso.online as the fixed baseline.

## Directly adopted

- Keep social and community areas separated by clear visitor intent instead of pushing everything through one compulsory global feed.
- Keep community discovery interest-based and understandable.
- Preserve the historical direction that visitors can choose feed types and community spaces while VIA stays transparent about what is live versus planned.
- Keep browser-local preferences lightweight where no account authority or blockchain write is needed.

## Implemented in this batch

VIA Communities now lets a visitor mark local interests such as Art & Photography, Music, DeSo & Builders, NFT & Collecting, Games & Quest and Open Community.

The selection is deliberately browser-local. It does not join communities, follow users, alter a DeSo profile or transmit sensitive-interest data to VIA.

## Historical ideas retained but not yet activated

- Real community membership and roles.
- Community posting and replies.
- Moderation tooling, mute/block/report flows and visibility rules.
- Notifications tied to community membership or followed discussions.
- Private or restricted communities.

These require explicit product rules plus current DeSo/security verification before VIA presents them as functional.

## Historical ideas not adopted as-is

- Silent derived-key actions without clear user awareness.
- Central platform-wallet signing or storage of seed phrases/private authority.
- Claims that social or community activity will propagate within a guaranteed number of seconds.
- Automatic profiling of sensitive interests or hidden feed changes based on inferred behaviour.

## Baseline rule

viadeso.online remains the baseline. Historical names and documents are inventory only. The public platform name is VIA.
