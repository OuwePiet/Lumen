# VIA Participation and Abuse Gate

## Fixed access model

VIA keeps one clear participation boundary:

- **Guest:** public view-only access. Navigation, search, reading, watching and listening may remain open.
- **DeSo authenticated:** participation may be enabled where the corresponding DeSo action has been safely implemented.
- **Sensitive action:** financial and blockchain writes require an additional preflight, explicit consent, signing and verification boundary.

VIA does not create a second Google/email participation identity beside DeSo.

## DeSo funding is not a VIA entrance fee

A current DeSo account may be subject to DeSo's own balance, funding, transaction or anti-abuse requirements. VIA may explain the current requirement and point to the appropriate DeSo funding/buy path after that path is verified.

VIA must not invent or hard-code its own minimum DeSo purchase merely to make an account look trusted. If DeSo changes its requirement, VIA should follow the verified current DeSo state rather than stale copy.

The economic hurdle is useful friction against cheap mass account creation, but payment is not proof that a person is honest.

## Login is not a trust badge

A DeSo login proves only the authority required by the verified authentication flow. It does not prove identity, honesty, reputation or safety.

Scammers can fund accounts, bots can operate funded accounts and legitimate accounts can be compromised. VIA therefore keeps behaviour protection above the login boundary.

## Behaviour protection

Before broad write access is activated, VIA should be able to contain at least:

- repeated or near-identical posting bursts;
- automated high-frequency actions;
- mass unsolicited contact;
- repeated malicious or deceptive links;
- impersonation attempts;
- suspicious upload/link patterns;
- attempts to bypass participation or signing controls;
- repeated abuse after a clear restriction.

Controls should be proportional. Rate limiting, cooldowns and temporary VIA-surface restrictions are preferred before unnecessarily broad measures. Financial/blockchain actions remain separately protected even for an account that has normal social access.

## No false safety claim

VIA must not present `DeSo logged in`, `funded`, `paid` or a displayed public key as a verified-person or trusted-user badge. Any future trust or verification label needs its own evidence and rules.

## Product shorthand

**Guest = view only.**

**DeSo login = may participate.**

**DeSo's own funding requirement = useful anti-bot friction, not proof of trust.**

**VIA behaviour controls = protection against spam, bots and scams.**

**Sensitive action = extra consent, signing and verification.**
