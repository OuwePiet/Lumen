# VIA Repeat

VIA Repeat is a low-cost visibility option for genuine DeSo creator and community content.

## Core rules

- Repeat applies to an existing creator post or the creator's own NFT content. It does not create duplicate blockchain posts or duplicate NFTs.
- Price: EUR 0.05 per scheduled repeat.
- The creator chooses one or more repeats per day and a period of 1 through 30 days.
- VIA shows the complete number of repeats and total price before confirmation.
- Customer-facing payment options for this service are EUR, USD and Bitcoin. DESO is not an advertising/payment currency for Repeat.
- Business advertising is not eligible for Repeat. Commercial campaigns use VIA Spotlight instead.
- A project-style account name does not automatically mean business. DeSo creator/community accounts can qualify when public account activity and the content support that classification.
- Having a DeSo account alone never grants creator classification or a lower commercial rate.
- Classification should consider the promoted content, public account behaviour/history and destination. Do not use a simple follower threshold and do not penalize genuine new or small creators.
- If Repeat content is detected as business advertising, VIA must refuse/pause Repeat and direct the advertiser to Spotlight rather than silently charging or publishing it.
- VIA should avoid excessive repetition to the same visitor and protect the normal feed experience.

## Examples

- 1 repeat/day for 30 days = 30 repeats = EUR 1.50.
- 2 repeats/day for 14 days = 28 repeats = EUR 1.40.
- 3 repeats/day for 30 days = 90 repeats = EUR 4.50.

Currency conversion to USD or Bitcoin must use a current checkout rate. The exact payable amount must be shown before payment.

## Safety and implementation boundary

This document defines product policy. Real payment, automatic publication, refunds, fraud controls, currency-rate handling and enforcement must not be presented as live until their production implementations have been reviewed and tested. VIA never silently signs blockchain transactions.
